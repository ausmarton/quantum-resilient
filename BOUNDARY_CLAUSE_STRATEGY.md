# Boundary Clause Reduction Strategy

## Current State
- Boundary clause appears 18 times with slight variations
- Pattern: "However, these findings should be interpreted within the controlled experimental framework..."
- Some instances are necessary, many are redundant

## Strategy
1. **Keep boundary clause** at:
   - End of major Analysis sections (4.2.1, 4.2.3, 4.2.5)
   - End of Objective interpretations (4.3.5)
   - End of Research Aim interpretation (4.4)
   - Key synthesis points in Conclusions

2. **Remove boundary clause** from:
   - Individual "Interpretation" paragraphs within subsections
   - Redundant instances in same section
   - Where context already implies experimental scope

3. **Use shorter version** where needed:
   - "within the experimental framework" (instead of full clause)
   - "under the evaluated conditions" (instead of full clause)

## Target: Reduce from 18 to ~6-8 instances
