# Writing Guidelines for Dissertation Updates

**Principle**: Be direct and let the content speak for itself. Avoid meta-language that describes what we're doing rather than just doing it.

---

## ❌ Avoid: Meta-Language Patterns

### 1. "This section/component/approach provides/ensures/enables..."
- ❌ "This section provides an overview..."
- ✅ Just start with the content

### 2. "This component addresses Objective X by..."
- ❌ "This component addresses Objective 1 by establishing criteria..."
- ✅ "Systematic literature analysis established criteria for algorithm selection."

### 3. "This ensures/enables/demonstrates that..."
- ❌ "This ensures that performance measurements capture..."
- ✅ "Performance measurements capture the same operational context..."

### 4. "The X component addressed this objective by..."
- ❌ "The benchmarking component addressed this objective by executing..."
- ✅ "Controlled experiments executed across multiple workload configurations..."

### 5. "This explicit mapping ensures that..."
- ❌ "This explicit mapping ensures that each research objective is addressed..."
- ✅ Remove entirely - the table speaks for itself

### 6. "Through this approach, the research contributed..."
- ❌ "Through this approach, the research contributed detailed performance analysis..."
- ✅ "The methodology utilised open-source implementations from the Open Quantum Safe (OQS) project."

---

## ✅ Good: Direct Language Patterns

### Instead of describing structure, just present it:
- ❌ "This section justifies the experimental methodology..."
- ✅ Start directly with the justification

### Instead of explaining what something does, just state what it is:
- ❌ "Table 3.1 maps each research objective to..."
- ✅ "Table 3.1: Mapping of Research Objectives to Methodology" (just the table)

### Instead of saying "this enables", integrate the benefit into the description:
- ❌ "This enables assessment of how deployment context affects performance"
- ✅ "enabling assessment of how deployment context affects performance" (as part of the description)

---

## Key Rules

1. **Remove self-referential statements** - Don't tell the reader what you're doing, just do it
2. **Remove explanatory meta-commentary** - If the content is clear, don't explain that it's clear
3. **Integrate benefits into descriptions** - Instead of "This ensures X", say "X" directly
4. **Let tables/figures speak for themselves** - Don't explain what they show if it's obvious
5. **Remove redundant objective mappings** - If you've already described what was done, don't repeat "this addressed objective X"

---

## Examples of Revisions Made

### Before:
"This section provides an overview of the research methodology..."

### After:
"The research methodology combines systematic literature analysis..."

---

### Before:
"This component addresses Objective 1 by establishing criteria for algorithm selection."

### After:
"Systematic literature analysis established criteria for algorithm selection."

---

### Before:
"This explicit mapping ensures that each research objective is addressed through appropriate measurement techniques..."

### After:
[Removed entirely - table is self-explanatory]

---

### Before:
"The framework validation activities (Section 3.3.4) confirmed that the framework produces measurements..."

### After:
"Framework validation activities (Section 3.3.4) confirmed that the framework produces measurements..."

---

**Remember**: Academic writing should be clear and direct. The reader should understand what was done from the description itself, not from explanations about what the description does.
