# Dissertation Improvement Plan

**Date**: 2025-12-15  
**Objective**: Improve dissertation quality through proper chapter separation, figure interpretation enhancement, and academic rigor

---

## Task Breakdown

### **Phase 1: Chapter Boundary Verification** (Priority: HIGH)

**Objective**: Ensure strict separation between Chapter 3 (Methodology) and Chapter 4 (Analysis) per guidance documents

**Tasks**:
1. **Audit Chapter 3 for Analysis content**
   - Identify any results, findings, or interpretations
   - Move any "WHAT was found" content to Chapter 4
   - Ensure all content describes "HOW research was conducted"

2. **Audit Chapter 4 for Methodology content**
   - Identify any methodological descriptions (HOW data was collected)
   - Move any "HOW" content to Chapter 3
   - Ensure all content describes "WHAT was found"

3. **Verify cross-references**
   - Ensure Chapter 4 references Chapter 3 appropriately
   - Remove redundant explanations in Chapter 4
   - Ensure methodology is explained once in Chapter 3

**Deliverable**: Clean chapter boundaries with no content conflation

---

### **Phase 2: Figure Interpretation Enhancement** (Priority: HIGH)

**Objective**: Transform figure descriptions into interpretations that support the narrative

**Current Issues**:
- Figures are being "described" rather than "interpreted"
- Captions may be too descriptive
- Text may narrate what's visible rather than explain significance

**Tasks**:
1. **Audit all figures in Chapter 4**
   - List all figures (4.1, 4.1a, 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, 4.5, 4.5a)
   - Identify descriptive vs interpretive language
   - Check caption quality

2. **Enhance figure interpretations**
   - Replace "Figure X shows..." with "Figure X indicates..."
   - Focus on what the figure reveals about performance
   - Link figure insights to research objectives
   - Remove redundant descriptions already visible in the figure

3. **Improve figure captions**
   - Ensure captions are interpretive, not just descriptive
   - Include key insights in captions
   - Reference relevant sections where appropriate

**Deliverable**: All figures interpreted with appropriate academic language

---

### **Phase 3: Content Quality Enhancement** (Priority: MEDIUM)

**Objective**: Improve academic rigor and clarity throughout

**Tasks**:
1. **Language refinement**
   - Ensure consistent use of past tense for completed work
   - Verify appropriate use of hedging language
   - Check for over-claiming or under-claiming

2. **Structure verification**
   - Verify three-layer structure (Result → Statistics → Interpretation) in Chapter 4
   - Ensure logical flow within sections
   - Check section coherence

3. **Cross-chapter coherence**
   - Verify objectives are addressed in both Methodology and Analysis
   - Ensure Conclusions synthesize rather than repeat
   - Check for contradictions

**Deliverable**: Enhanced academic quality throughout

---

### **Phase 4: Assessment Guide Review** (Priority: LOW)

**Objective**: Review assessment guide for any pre-TMA04 guidance still relevant

**Tasks**:
1. **Review assessment guide**
   - Identify pre-TMA04 guidance
   - Determine relevance to current stage
   - Apply any applicable guidance

**Deliverable**: Assessment guide compliance verified
